Revisit History · About

Revisit History is a reflective programme by Acces for accessible knowledge-sharing. It revisits difficult histories one subject at a time — through walking tours, bike rides, and dialogue in motion — engaging the past to make the present more understandable and open to dialogue. Its current subject is A Colonial Presence.

Revisit History · The Subject

A Colonial Presence

The first subject under Revisit History — Antwerp and the colonial century, read through the visible and invisible traces the era pressed into the city's everyday surfaces.

The approach doesn't lean on traditional historical narrative; it starts from connectedness, reflection, and structural insight — making complex themes accessible without simplifying them, and inviting active engagement, dialogue, and reinterpretation.

Subjects under study
  1. N°01 A Colonial Presence On view now

Further subjects will join Revisit History in time — each a new lens on a difficult past.

Led on the ground by guides who hold space for plural readings of the city. Conversations stay open; conclusions are not the point.
